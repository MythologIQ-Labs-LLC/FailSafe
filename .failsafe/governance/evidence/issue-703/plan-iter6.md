- non_goals: server-side review semantics, payloads or authorization (`DeliveryRoute.ts`, `deliveryReview.ts` untouched); persistence of drafts across reloads; per-intent storage of more than one form or note; any version bump, tag or release
- exclusions: protected workflows under `.github/workflows/**`; two pre-existing file-length findings that this plan reduces but does not remove: `delivery-assessment-renderer.js` (482 lines by `wc -l` at origin/main, 433 after this plan) and `delivery-assessment-renderer.test.ts` (380 at origin/main, 296 after this plan), both over 250; the pre-existing nested ternaries (renderer lines 159, 301, 425, 426 at the candidate; one moved unchanged into the fixture at line 40; none added).
